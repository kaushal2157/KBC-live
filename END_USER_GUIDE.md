# 🎮 How to Use the KBC Quiz App

## Start Here

This guide shows you how to use the KBC Quiz Application in **5 minutes or less**.

---

## 📱 Step 1: Open the App

1. Ask your administrator to give you the app link
2. Click the link on your computer or phone
3. You'll see a login screen

---

## 🔑 Step 2: Log In

**Username**: `client`  
**Password**: `client@123`

1. Type the username
2. Type the password
3. Click **"Access Application"**

## 🎯 Step 3: Use the App

After logging in, you'll see a menu with these options:

### 🏠 Home
- See the welcome screen
- View general information

### 🎓 Quiz
- Take the quiz
- Answer questions
- See your score

### 👥 Contestants
- View list of people who took the quiz
- See their scores

### ⚙️ Settings
- Change your preferences

---

## 📝 Taking the Quiz

1. Click **"Quiz"** in the menu
2. You'll see a question on screen
3. Read the question carefully
4. Click the answer you think is correct
5. Click **"Next Question"** to continue
6. Repeat until you finish all questions
7. See your final score

---

## 🚪 Step 4: Log Out

When done using the app:

1. Look for **"Logout"** button (usually top right)
2. Click it
3. You'll return to the login screen

---

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: I forgot my password**
- Ask your administrator for the correct password

**Q: Can I retake the quiz?**
- Yes! Log out and log in again to take it again

**Q: Can I see my previous scores?**
- Yes! Go to **Contestants** section to see all scores

**Q: How many questions are there?**
- It depends on the quiz. You'll see the score when done.

**Q: Does the app work on my phone?**
- Yes! It works on phones, tablets, and computers

**Q: Will my answers be saved?**
- Your score is saved and shown in the Contestants list

---

## ⚡ Quick Tips

✅ **Click carefully** - Once you select an answer, it's submitted  
✅ **Read fully** - Read the entire question before answering  
✅ **Take your time** - No time limit  
✅ **Use good internet** - Stable connection recommended  
✅ **Don't refresh** - Refreshing might reset your progress  

---

## 🆘 Need Help?

If something doesn't work:

1. **Try refreshing** - Press F5 on keyboard or reload button
2. **Clear your browser** - Close the tab and start over
3. **Check internet** - Make sure you're connected
4. **Contact admin** - Ask your administrator for support

---

## 🎉 Tips for Success

1. **Read carefully** - Understand the question completely
2. **Think before clicking** - You can't change your answer
3. **Relax** - There's no time pressure
4. **Focus** - Minimize distractions
5. **Take a break** - If needed, log out and come back
